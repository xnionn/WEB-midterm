Note 01 / On getting started / 3 min read

The blank page
isn’t a test.

There’s a particular kind of hesitation that arrives with a new notebook. The first mark suddenly feels far too important.

Give the page a smaller job. It doesn’t have to hold your best idea. It can hold five lines, a rubbing of a leaf, or a list of things you noticed on the way home.

A useful constraint makes the first move easier: one colour, two minutes, only shapes with corners. Choose a rule that feels playful, then let the page surprise you.

When the timer ends, resist deciding whether the result is good. Ask a more useful question: what would I like to try next?
