    Come as you are.

    Alone, with a friend, tired after class. There’s a seat for every version of you.

  2. 02

    Try something small.

    Make a print. Learn a game. Draw a bad chair. You don’t have to be good to enjoy it.

    Take a story home.

    Leave with something you made and someone you actually talked to.
