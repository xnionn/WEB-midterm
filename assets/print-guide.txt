OFFLINE / FIELD KIT 01
THE FOUR-PANEL PAUSE

20 minutes. One sheet. Four small ideas.

YOU WILL NEED
[ ] One sheet of scrap paper
[ ] A pen or pencil
[ ] Something small to look at
[ ] Twenty quiet minutes

Fold your paper in half, then in half again. Unfold it.
Each of the four panels gets one small experiment.
The timings are a guide. There is no need to be exact.

01 / NOTICE / 5 MINUTES
Choose an ordinary object nearby. Write five details you
usually miss: its edges, weight, texture, shadow, or the
sound it makes. A key, a leaf, or your favourite mug works.

02 / DRAW / 5 MINUTES
Sketch the object with one continuous line. Keep your pen
on the page. A recognisable drawing is optional; careful
looking is the point. Let the line wander a little.

03 / IMAGINE / 5 MINUTES
Give your object another life. A mug becomes a tiny planet.
A key becomes a fish. Draw or describe the new possibility.
Follow the first odd idea.

04 / KEEP / 5 MINUTES
Write one sentence about what you noticed. Date the page,
fold it up, and keep it somewhere you will find it again.
"Today I noticed..." is enough.

There is no finished look to aim for. Four panels filled in
your own way count as a good twenty minutes.

TRY IT AGAIN
Choose an object from your pocket, something from outside,
or a thing you have owned for years. Compare the pages,
not their quality: what changed in the way you looked?
