OFFLINE / FIELD KIT 01

The four-panel pause.

Fold your paper in half, then in half again. Unfold it: each of the four panels gets one small experiment.

Panel 1 of 4
  1. 015 minutes

    Notice.

    Choose an ordinary object nearby. Write five details you usually miss: its edges, weight, texture, shadow, or the sound it makes.

    A key. A leaf. Your favourite mug.
  2. 025 minutes

    Draw.

    Sketch the object with one continuous line. Keep your pen on the page. A recognisable drawing is optional; careful looking is the point.

    Let the line wander a little.
  3. 035 minutes

    Imagine.

    Give your object another life. A mug becomes a tiny planet. A key becomes a fish. Draw or describe the new possibility.

    Follow the first odd idea.
  4. 045 minutes

    Keep.

    Write one sentence about what you noticed. Date the page, fold it up, and keep it somewhere you’ll find it again.

    “Today I noticed…” is enough.

There is no finished look to aim for. Four panels filled in your own way count as a good twenty minutes.

Make a habit of a small pause

Same kit.
Different day.

Try it again with an object from your pocket, something from outside, or a thing you’ve owned for years. Compare the pages, not their quality: what changed in the way you looked?

When you’d like some company, bring your paper to a gathering. A small experiment makes a good conversation starter.
