Our very short manifesto

Four things we stand by.

Keep these. Skip the pressure.
  1. 01

    Start before you feel ready.

    You don’t need to call yourself creative. A wonky first attempt is a perfectly good place to begin.

  2. 02

    Use what’s already around.

    Scrap paper, an old magazine, a pencil borrowed from a friend. Simple materials leave more room for ideas.

  3. 03

    Leave comparison at the door.

    There’s no best work at the table. Share your process if you want to; keeping it to yourself is fine too.

  4. 04

    Take something small with you.

    A folded zine, a better question, or the feeling of having tried. The result doesn’t have to be a masterpiece.

A table, not a timetable

How an evening
comes together.

Enough structure to get started. Enough freedom to make it your own.

  1. 00–15

    Arrive & settle in

    Find a seat, pick up your materials, and meet the people beside you.

  2. 15–75

    Follow the prompt

    The host shows a starting point. You take it in whatever direction feels interesting.

  3. 75–90

    Share & pack up

    Show a detail, ask a question, or simply listen. Leave the table ready for its next idea.
